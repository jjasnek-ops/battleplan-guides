## Which Battleplan does this site cover?

This is the World War II strategy game on Steam under App ID **3743140**, developed by Foolish Mortals Games Inc. and published by Slitherine Ltd. Its focus is commanding military operations on maps. The name also belongs to unrelated games, a television series and mechanics in other strategy games.

The [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3743140/Battleplan/) lists October 12, 2026 as the release date. Use that app identifier when checking guides or downloads. Start with our [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) and [unit roles](/units/unit-types/) for this game's command systems.

## What is in the launch campaign?

The October 2 launch overview describes **11 historical campaign missions and 2 tutorial levels**, spanning D-Day through Operation Varsity. The campaign carries an army forward between battles. The publisher's game page identifies an Allied campaign and German play in individual scenarios.

## What changed after the demo?

The launch overview explicitly describes Zone Orders, an expanded Army Constructor and improvements to reconnaissance, combined arms, artillery, air operations, defensive fortifications and the interface. A February demo tutorial should therefore be treated as version-specific. Do not assume its layout or restrictions describe every release-build interaction.

Read [Zone Orders](/guides/zone-orders/) for the documented area controls and the [Army Constructor guide](/units/army-constructor/) for formation preparation. The tutorials inside your installed build provide the current interface context.
